# Kolt Kase Inc - Complete E-Commerce Landing Page

A production-grade, multi-section e-commerce landing page built for **Kolt Kase Inc**, combining the authentic 1:1 visual design of Image 1 for the Hero Section with the full vertical layout hierarchy of Image 2 for the rest of the store.

## Architecture & Section Hierarchy

1. **Global Pinned Navigation**: Glassmorphic header with K-mark logo, navigation links, quick search, and interactive shopping bag drawer trigger with dynamic badge count.
2. **Section 1: Hero Section (Exact Match to Image 1)**:
   - Minimalist K-mark vector emblem + `K O L T   K A S E   I N C` top-left.
   - Bold italic sans-serif headline: `ENGINEERED` in dark navy (`#083B5C`) + `FOR MORE` (`MORE` in radiant metallic orange `#F88E4C`).
   - Spaced-out uppercase sub-headline: `ULTRA-DURABLE. ULTRA-LIGHT. BUILT TO LAST.`
   - 3-Column feature highlights row with vertical divider lines (Shield, Feather, Lightning).
   - Action buttons: Metallic orange pill (`SHOP THE COLLECTION →`) and transparent dark outline pill (`LEARN MORE`).
   - Dynamic 3D exploded case stack hovering at 45° with floating chrome droplets, glowing orange rings, and rocky terrain base.
3. **Section 2: Value Proposition Strip**: 4-column horizontal icon bar on a crisp warm/light strip (Free Shipping Worldwide, 100-Day Drop Guarantee, Custom Engraving Available, 24/7 Premium Support).
4. **Section 3: Top Selling & Custom Designs Grid**:
   - Header: `BEST-SELLING CASES & CUSTOM DESIGNS` with filter tabs (All, Carbon Fiber, Armor TPU, Graphic Prints, Minimalist).
   - 4-column borderless product cards with floating phone case mockups, device compatibility, star ratings, prices, and pill-style "Add to Bag" buttons.
5. **Section 4: Full-Width Feature Banner**:
   - Dark carbon twill texture with glowing orange light leak.
   - Headline: `PRECISION ENGINEERED PROTECTION`.
   - Technical stats: 14FT Drop Certified, 0.01mm CNC Precision, 100% MagSafe & Qi2, 34g Featherweight.

6. **Section 5: Custom Case Collage & Category Highlight**:
   - Left 2x2 Matrix: Tactical Series, Ultra-Slim Carbon, Topo & Geo Prints, Minimalist Matte.

7. **Section 6: Four-Layer Tech Architecture Breakdown**: Exploded kinetic energy dispersion breakdown with nanoscale oleophobic glaze, 3K carbon fiber armor, honeycomb chamber, and beveled TPU cushion.
8. **Section 7: Verified Customer Reviews & Social Proof**: 4.9/5 overall rating badge from 18,400+ reviews with verified buyer cards and phone model tags.
9. **Section 8: Technical FAQ Accordion**: Expandable pre-purchase questions on MagSafe, drop warranty, scratch resistance, and international shipping.
10. **Section 9: Slide-Out Cart Drawer**: Slide-in cart panel with item list, quantity adjusters, free shipping progress bar, subtotal calculation, and instant dispatch checkout.
11. **Section 10: Luxury Footer**: Deep dark carbon navy footer with newsletter discount prompt, company links, and payment method badges.
